Hello

I am Jordon Cameron

A student, programmer, and artist


Personality Traits

Calm

Having a level-headed attitude in a high stress environment has been vital to my development as a student.

Compassionate

No man is an island, and as such, being a person who values the concerns of others has made me more approachable. This is valuable in an environment where teamwork and camaraderie are often needed.

Creative

No matter the subject area, creativity is an important part of my personality. It not only helps with my artistic pursuits, but my problem solving skills.

Hobbies

Writing

I am hugely passionate about writing - especially fiction - as it is a medium that allows me to articulate thoughts that do not easily come from my speech alone.

Art

Art is a broad label that covers many of my interests.
Namely: Painting, Drawing, Animation, Game Design, and Graphic Design
The arts are my therapy. They are the place where I can wildly experiment without a care for principle or improvement. It relaxes my mind after a long day, and resets me.

Watching YouTube

A casual passtime, that is not reseved merely for content consumption, but also for expanding my knowledge. Video essays and niche D.I.Y projects are my favourite kinds of videos.

Dominant Emotions

Joy

In such a nihilistic world, remaining joyful is important to keeping a level-head.

Curiosity

Restricting myself to one thing for a prolonged period of time leaves me unfulfilled. Exploring new subjects and expanding my horizons not only keeps me fulfilled, but also sharp.

Timidness

This is a negative, and in my opinion irrational emotion I have where I overthink certain social interactions. These may be nothing to the everyday Jamaican, like:

  • Setting an appointment
  • Ordering food
  • Getting a haircut at the barber

but I often overthink these situations to the point where I don't pursue them.
